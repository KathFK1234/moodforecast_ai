Privacy

You can check the weather without giving any details. Places you search for are sent to our weather and map services, and your browser remembers the last one.

Daily email alerts

If you subscribe, we keep your email address, your location and your chosen activity, and use them only to send your alerts. Your browser remembers the subscription too.

Who handles it

The site runs on Railway, and Brevo sends the emails. Searches go to Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap. We don't sell your details or use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Unsubscribing deletes your details from our database straight away.
  • A request nobody confirms is deleted after 48 hours.
  • Brevo keeps its own record of emails it has sent for a limited time.

Questions

Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or ask a question on GitHub.
